# zeropdf logo

The mark reduces a generated PDF to two structural layers: a strong outer document silhouette and a smaller page held inside it. The nested construction suggests a self-contained document engine, while the two compact rules keep the document reading clear at favicon size.

- `logo-mark.svg` is the primary brand-green mark for light, dark, or transparent surfaces.
- `logo-mark-mono.svg` inherits `currentColor` for UI, print, and reversed use.
- `logo-app-icon.svg` is the plated square source for app and social icons.

Keep clear space equal to roughly one quarter of the mark width. Use the standalone mark at 16 px or larger and the plated icon at 32 px or larger. Do not add outlines, shadows, gradients, or text inside the mark.
